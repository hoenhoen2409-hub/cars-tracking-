Each brand's monthly share of the sum of all tracked brands. Independent of the Month from/to filter in §1 above -- always shows the full history.

EV (VinFast) Share of Total Market

VinFast is used as a proxy for EV volume since it's Vietnam's dominant battery-EV seller and the only tracked brand that's 100% electric. Other brands' hybrid/EV models aren't broken out separately in this dataset, so this understates true electrified volume.

Annual Sales by Brand

The current year is year-to-date through the latest confirmed month; its growth column (if shown) only compares full calendar years with 12 confirmed months on both ends.

Not yet available from this data

This tracker records total monthly units per brand, not per model or powertrain, so a Hybrid / Gasoline / Battery-EV split within a brand (e.g. Toyota's hybrid mix) can't be derived from it. VAMA's own passenger / commercial / special-purpose vehicle segmentation is also not tracked monthly here (it was only pulled once, from the raw VAMA PDFs, to get July–Aug 2026's Thaco sub-brand figures). Both would need additional scraping of VAMA's segment tables or brand-level model/powertrain disclosures.
